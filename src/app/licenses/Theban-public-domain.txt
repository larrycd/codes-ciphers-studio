CC Theban (fonts/CCTheban.otf)

The 23 Theban letter outlines come from the "Theban glyph A.svg" ... "Theban glyph Z.svg" files on
Wikimedia Commons (category: Theban alphabet), drawn by User:Fuzzypeg and released into the public domain.
The Theban alphabet itself was first printed by Johannes Trithemius (Polygraphia, 1518) and by
Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa (De occulta philosophia, 1533).

The outlines were scaled and assembled into a font for Codes & Ciphers Studio by Larry Dalton.
Theban has no place in Unicode, so the letters sit at U+E100 to U+E116 (Private Use Area), in the order
A B C D E F G H I K L M N O P Q R S T V X Y Z.
